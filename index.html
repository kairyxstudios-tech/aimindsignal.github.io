MindSignal Privacy Policy

Your behavioral wellness data belongs to you.

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

Introduction

MindSignal ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the MindSignal mobile application ("App").

Package Name: com.aistudio.mindsignal.pvywdg

MindSignal is designed around a local-first architecture. Behavioral analysis is performed on your device and your wellness data is not stored on MindSignal servers.

1. Local Processing & Data Ownership

MindSignal is built to maximize privacy and user control.

  • Behavioral analysis occurs locally on your device.
  • No user account is required.
  • MindSignal does not maintain a cloud database of your wellness history.
  • You may delete your stored information at any time.
  • Your data remains under your control.

2. Information Processed by the App

MindSignal may process the following categories of information locally:

  • Mood check-ins
  • Energy assessments
  • Sleep self-reports
  • Wellness logs
  • Behavioral trend indicators
  • Activity metrics
  • Breathing exercise progress
  • Dashboard visualizations

These records are used solely to generate personal wellness insights.

3. Simulated Behavioral Indicators

MindSignal uses privacy-preserving behavioral indicators to help users understand wellness patterns.

  • Relative Home Time Trends
  • Typing Rhythm Changes
  • Physical Activity Variations
  • Messaging Activity Trends
  • Late-Night Screen Activity
  • Application Variety Metrics

MindSignal does NOT collect:

  • Message contents
  • Contact lists
  • Passwords
  • Raw keystrokes
  • Private conversations
  • Detailed GPS history

4. AI Companion

MindSignal includes an optional AI companion feature intended to provide supportive wellness conversations and reflective guidance.

The AI companion requires an active internet connection.

Messages sent to the AI companion may be processed by third-party AI providers solely for generating responses.

Core wellness analysis continues to function offline even when the AI companion is unavailable.

5. Permissions

Depending on enabled functionality, MindSignal may request:

  • Internet Access
  • Notification Permission
  • Activity Recognition Permission
  • Other permissions required for wellness-related features

Permissions are requested only when necessary.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

MindSignal may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect advertising identifiers, device information, diagnostic data, and advertising performance information according to Google's policies.

Users may manage advertising preferences through Android settings.

7. Data Retention

Wellness records remain stored locally on your device until:

  • You delete them manually.
  • You use the data wipe feature.
  • You uninstall the application.

8. Security

MindSignal relies on Android's application sandboxing, local storage protections, and security mechanisms to help safeguard stored data.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, but we implement reasonable security measures.

9. Children's Privacy

MindSignal is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Updates become effective when published on this page.
